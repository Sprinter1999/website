I am a Staff Engineer at KargoBot. My current work centers on real-time, reliability-oriented end-to-end autonomous driving models for trucks, together with the supporting training and deployment infrastructure, and on longer-horizon research in autonomous driving, multi-modal foundation models and agentic systems.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at the Institute of Computing Technology, CAS (2026) under the supervision of Prof. Min Liu, Assoc. Prof. Yuwei Wang and Assoc. Prof. Sheng Sun, and my B.E. at BUPT (2021), followed by work at AMD, Li Auto and Meituan on visual-inertial SLAM, end-to-end planning and advertising algorithms, respectively. Discussions and collaborations are always welcome.
